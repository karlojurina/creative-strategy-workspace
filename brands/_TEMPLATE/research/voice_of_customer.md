# Voice of Customer - <Brand Name>

The brand's phrase bank: real customer language, verbatim, sorted by what kind of ammunition it is. Research collects the language (`research/raw/` holds the full material); THIS file is where the best lines get promoted so every script and static can pull from one place.

**The rules:**
- **Verbatim only.** Their exact words, typos and all. The moment you paraphrase, the gold is gone.
- **Source every snippet.** `- "the line" · source (our reviews / competitor reviews / our ad comments / reddit / survey / social) · DD-MM-YYYY`
- **Mark repeats.** When you see the same phrase again from a new person, add `(x2)`, `(x3)`... to the snippet instead of duplicating it. Recurrence = the market telling you what its real language is.
- **Written vs spoken.** Everything in here was TYPED. On a static ad, a verbatim ships as-is. In a video script, voice it, don't paste it - keep their words, re-cadence for the mouth (see `resources/copywriting/spoken_voice.md`).
- Filled by `/market-research` on day one, topped up by `/research-pulse` weekly, read by `/video-copy` and `/static-copy` before every draft.
