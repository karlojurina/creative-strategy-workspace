# Raw research - the append-forever layer

Everything mined from the real world lands here, organized by source, dated, never rewritten. The synthesis docs one level up (`market-research.md`, `avatars.md`, `desires.md`) are DISTILLED from this layer - raw is the river, synthesis is the map of the river. When something in here contradicts or outgrows a synthesis doc, flag it and update the doc.

**File naming:** `DD-MM-YYYY_<what-it-is>.md` · quotes verbatim · a source link on everything.

## The folders, and the lens for each

Our own material and competitor material get read with DIFFERENT eyes - that difference is the whole game:

| Folder | What lands here | The lens |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| `reviews/ours/` | Reviews of OUR product | Hunt **emotional responses** - lines that could become a headline or an angle |
| `reviews/competitors/` | Reviews of competitor products | Hunt what people **dislike** - every complaint is a positioning opportunity for us |
| `ad-comments/ours/` | Comments under our own ads | Emotional reactions, objections to answer, lines worth reusing (feed them through the comment-miner prompt) |
| `ad-comments/competitors/` | Comments under competitor ads | What their audience pushes back on and what excites them - the gaps we can take |
| `reddit-forums/` | Threads and discussions in the niche | Authentic language, failed solutions, "holy grail" wishes |
| `social/` | Popping posts, trends, viral content in the niche | What formats and angles the market is rewarding right now |
| `surveys/` | Post-purchase and customer surveys | Why they REALLY bought, in their own words |
