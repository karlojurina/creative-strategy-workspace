# Winning Ads - Real Case Studies

Real winning ads from real brands, broken down start to finish: how the research found the idea, how the script was built, how the ad performed, and what got learned. These are not templates to copy - the products and audiences are not yours. They are the PROCESS, shown working. Read them the way you'd watch over someone's shoulder.

| Case study | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| [flowpouch_story.md](flowpouch_story.md) | The full arc, dated: research -> idea -> Eugene Schwartz craft upgrade -> brief rewrite -> top spender next day -> iteration into a bigger market. **Read this one first.** |
| [carepod_stainless_steel.md](carepod_stainless_steel.md) | Test -> learning -> chase the WHY with research -> new-mechanism iteration in a new format. Awareness + sophistication used as a working tool. |
| [dog_friendly_adoption_story.md](dog_friendly_adoption_story.md) | An unaware-level emotional story ad: mining the research doc, imitating proven viral content, selling stealthily. |
| [metaglow_losing_to_winning.md](metaglow_losing_to_winning.md) | The learnings masterclass: why an almost-winner died, how the diagnosis was made, and the rewrite that beat it. Text-on-screen theory, Hemingway rule, anti-hype angle. |

## When to pull these

- Before your first `/video-copy` for a new brand - to calibrate what "every line has a purpose" actually looks like.
- When an ad of yours shows potential and dies - read Metaglow.
- When your market is drowning in identical claims - read CarePod and FlowPouch (the mechanism play).
- When you need an unaware ad - read Dog Friendly Co.
- When a winner dies and you're deciding what's next - read the FlowPouch iteration, then the [iteration playbook](../iteration-playbook.md).

Every one of these ends the same way: a learning written down and fed into the next ad. That is the loop your growth guide runs on.
