# **Comment Miner Prompt**

Hey Claude, I just copied the Facebook/Instagram comment section under one of our ads and I need you to analyze these comments for me. Please:

## **Organization Instructions**

1.  Remove all usernames and personal identifiers completely
2.  Create a professionally-formatted document with clear hierarchical structure
3.  Organize comments into main categories with descriptive headings (e.g., "Product Ingredients Questions", "Usage Instructions", "Effectiveness Feedback")
4.  Create focused subcategories that group very similar topics together
5.  Ensure each category has no more than 8-10 comments to maintain readability
6.  Present categories in order of marketing importance, with the most valuable customer insights first

## **Formatting Rules**

1.  Format primary comments with "●" bullets
2.  Format replies with "○" indentation
3.  Use clear heading hierarchy with proper spacing between sections
4.  If a comment thread is lengthy (more than 3 replies), only include the most informative exchanges
5.  Group similar questions/comments together even if they weren't sequential in the original
6.  Create a clean, easy-to-scan document that highlights patterns

## **Content Guidelines**

1.  Preserve EXACTLY what people said including slang, errors, and emojis
2.  Include ALL substantive comments about:
      
      - Product questions and concerns
      - Personal experiences (positive and negative)
      - Objections or skepticism
      - Testimonials and results
      - Feature requests or suggestions
3.  Omit generic comments with no informational value (like "following" or single emojis)
4.  For long threads about the same topic, summarize duplicate points

## **Analysis Section**

After organizing all comments, provide a comprehensive analysis that includes:

1.  **Overall Sentiment Patterns****  
      
    **
      
      - Clear breakdown of positive/negative/neutral sentiment proportions
      - Identification of emotionally charged topics
2.  **Audience Insights****  
      
    **
      
      - Demographic patterns based on language and context
      - Different user segments and their specific interests/concerns
      - Level of product understanding/education
3.  **Key Questions & Concerns****  
      
    **
      
      - Top 5 most frequent questions or objections
      - Areas of confusion or misunderstanding
      - Scientific or technical concerns
4.  **Pain Points & Desires****  
      
    **
      
      - Clear statement of problems users want solved
      - "Before and after" scenarios mentioned
      - Explicit and implicit needs
5.  **Social Sharing Patterns****  
      
    **
      
      - Reasons people tag friends
      - Content that generates engagement
      - Elements that make the product "shareable"
6.  **Response to Product Features****  
      
    **
      
      - Features generating most excitement
      - Features causing confusion or skepticism
      - Missing features users request
7.  **Most Valuable Testimonials****  
      
    **
      
      - Direct quotes from compelling success stories
      - Recurring patterns in positive experiences
      - Third-party validation elements
8.  **Marketing Strategy Insights****  
      
    **
      
      - Language patterns to adopt in marketing copy
      - Topics requiring more education/clarification
      - Positioning opportunities against competitors
      - Audience segments to prioritize
