# Copywriting

Reference material for writing and assembling copy. The mindset lives in the [MANIFESTO](../../MANIFESTO.md); the frameworks live in [breakthrough-advertising/](../breakthrough-advertising/); this folder holds the working craft, guide by guide.

| Guide | What it holds | Pull it when |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| [script_principles.md](script_principles.md) | The order of operations and the structure of a persuasive script: hook -> meet them -> educate -> mechanism -> bridge -> product. The one-line law: educate before the product | Writing or reviewing ANY script · before your first `/video-copy` or `/static-copy` |
| [hooks.md](hooks.md) | The first 3-5 seconds: the two jobs (impossible to scroll past, impossible to misread who it's for), the loop-opening moves, batches and hook variations, the hook-rate trap | Writing hook variations · a batch needs its 4 openers · an ad gets no spend |
| [retention.md](retention.md) | Holding attention after the hook: every line earns the next, the silent dialogue, open loops, the seconds 4-10 bridge, educate-to-hold, pacing and emphasis, ad length | Hold rate is leaking · the script drags · deciding how long an ad should be |
| [writing_for_your_avatar.md](writing_for_your_avatar.md) | Style follows the person: the grandma test, calibrating bluntness, YOUful copy, stealing their language, matching awareness level, the pre-draft checklist | Starting copy for a new avatar · the draft sounds generic · tone feels off |

The grab-and-use prompts (comment miner, ideas-from-reviews, hook ideas) live in [../prompts/](../prompts/). Real examples of all of this working: [../winning-ads/](../winning-ads/).
