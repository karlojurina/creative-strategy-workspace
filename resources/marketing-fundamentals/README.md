# Marketing Fundamentals - The Foundation Library

The timeless layer under everything else in this workspace: how the ads game works, why people buy, the environment your ads live in, the numbers, the research craft, and the mindset that makes any of it stick. Distilled from Karlo's EcomU foundation modules. No flashy tactics - fundamentals, because fundamentals are timeless.

**Reading order for a fresh start:** how ads work -> buyer psychology -> scroll psychology, then the rest as the work demands them.

| Guide | What it holds | Pull it when |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| [how_ads_work.md](how_ads_work.md) | Marketing defined, why paid ads, direct response vs branded, the seven-step loop, who owns what, the testing doctrine | You're new to the game · you need the big picture back · explaining the process to anyone |
| [buyer_psychology.md](buyer_psychology.md) | Desire (channel it, never create it), grading desires, the two products, selling the transformation, "this is literally me" | Choosing an angle or desire · a draft is product-focused instead of person-focused |
| [scroll_psychology.md](scroll_psychology.md) | The slot machine, the lazy brain, "how hard does the brain have to work?", clarity beats clever, look native | Any creative decision about format or clarity · an ad feels like an ad |
| [metrics_and_diagnosis.md](metrics_and_diagnosis.md) | The metric funnel with formulas + benchmarks, campaign/ad set/ad, ABO vs CBO, creative is the new targeting | Reading the ad account · judging soft vs hard metrics · before your first learnings pass |
| [research_methods.md](research_methods.md) | The manual research playbook: niche content, burner accounts, comment mining, Reddit, 3-star Amazon gold, your own ad comments | Starting research on any brand · you're stuck with no ad ideas · calibrating what /market-research should find |
| [operator_mindset.md](operator_mindset.md) | Skill stacking, learning = changed behavior, staying ahead of AI, the lock-in system, you vs you, pull over push | Motivation dips · you're consuming instead of doing · deciding what work to take |
