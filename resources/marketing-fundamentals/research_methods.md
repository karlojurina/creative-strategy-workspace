# Research Methods - Building the Knowledge Base In Your Head

Research is the first step of every ad and the source of every winning idea in this workspace's case studies - the FlowPouch mechanism came from READING the research document, the Dog Friendly story came from mining one, CarePod's why came from Reddit threads. This doc is the manual craft. `/market-research` and `/research-pulse` automate the collection - but the method below is what you must be able to do by hand, because of one rule:

**Do it manually first.** "If you do this manually, that is when you truly start building a knowledge base inside of your head. Whereas if you use AI to just generate a research document, you're never gonna truly understand who you're selling to." AI collects; YOU absorb. The ideas spark while you're reading, not while the document generates.

## The playbook

**1. Watch niche content.** Search the niche's keywords on TikTok/Instagram/YouTube and study what the audience actually watches - formats, faces, pacing, thumbnails. Your ad has to look like something they'd watch anyway.

**2. Run a burner account.** Reverse-engineer the feed of your ideal avatar: follow what they'd follow, engage like they would, and let the algorithm show you their world. You're seeing their feed, which means you're seeing your competition for attention.

**3. Mine comments on viral posts.** The comment section of a viral niche post is the market talking to itself: questions, objections, "I need this," "wish I'd known," who they tag and why. Group comments by theme; note the exact phrasing of interest and skepticism (the [comment-miner prompt](../prompts/comment-miner.md) helps at volume).

**4. Reddit - "this is honestly the method."** Find the niche subreddits and read. How they describe the problem, what they've tried, what failed, the jargon they use, the emotions underneath. Reddit is where people are honest. Whenever you're stuck with no idea what ad to make next - go read Reddit for an hour.

**5. Amazon reviews - and 3-star reviews are the gold.** Five-star reviews give you desire language, one-star gives you objections, but three-star reviews give you BOTH sides in one honest paragraph. Read the good ones and the bad ones for your product's category and the competitors'.

**6. Facebook groups.** Niche groups are unfiltered customer conversations at scale - problems, recommendations, warnings, in the avatar's own words.

**7. Your own ad comments.** Feedback from the market in real time, on YOUR messaging specifically. Objections found here become hooks ("comment-as-hook" - use them verbatim). Stalk the commenter profiles: these are real buyers showing you who they are.

Everything you collect gets filed in the brand's `research/raw/` layer (ours vs competitors, different reading lenses) so it compounds instead of evaporating - and the weekly `/research-pulse` keeps it flowing.

## What you're hunting for

Research is **claim-hunting** plus **language-hunting**:

- The desires, ranked by intensity / staying power / scope - and the ONE dominant one.
- The problems, in the exact words customers use for them (5-7 verbatim examples per desire/problem).
- What they've already tried and how it failed them (so you never pitch what already burned them).
- The objections - and the belief gaps behind them.
- The emotional state: frustrated? skeptical? hopeful? desperate? (This calibrates the whole tone - see [../copywriting/writing_for_your_avatar.md](../copywriting/writing_for_your_avatar.md).)
- Hooks and formats already proven viral in the niche (patterns, not sentences).
- Awareness level and market sophistication - the two lenses that decide what kind of ad can even work ([BA summary](../breakthrough-advertising/breakthrough_advertising_summary.md)).

## How AI fits

After the manual reps: point the AI at the same sources to widen coverage, ask for source links, and read the best threads yourself. Before writing copy, make the AI re-research the specific avatar so it picks up their language fresh (the warm-up step from the [Metaglow case study](../winning-ads/metaglow_losing_to_winning.md)). And feed everything back into the brand's research docs - the document is a reusable asset that pays for itself on every future batch.

Aim the output at **3-4 strong angles, not one perfect one, not twenty mediocre ones.**
