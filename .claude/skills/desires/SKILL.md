# /desires

Build the desire doc for a brand from its research.

## Workflow

0. **Open with the explanation, then wait.** Tell the user what this produces and why: desires are what the market ALREADY wants - you never create desire, you channel it toward the product (manifesto). This skill pulls the desires visible in the research, writes each as a first-person line in the customer's language with the evidence behind it, and the doc lands at `research/desires.md`. Every concept card in the growth guide names the one desire it is built on, so these go into the guide too. Ask if they're ready.
1. Read `MANIFESTO.md` and the brand's `research/market-research.md`. If there is no research doc yet, tell the user to run `/market-research` first. Also pull in reviews, surveys, and anything else in `research/` - every desire must be grounded in real customer language.
2. From the research, identify at least 10 mass desires. Present them and ask the user to confirm or adjust - their call, not yours. Wait for their answer.
3. Once confirmed, say what happens now in one line (each desire written up with its psychological core, how the product fulfills it, and the customer evidence), then write the doc using the method below.
4. Save to `brands/<slug>/research/desires.md` (the desire doc). This feeds the growth guide - the confirmed desires also go into the Desires section of `growth-guide.md`, verbatim.

Remember the manifesto: you channel desire, you do not create it. These are desires the market already has.

## The method

Based on all the customer comments, reviews, and demographic information gathered about this brand and product, identify 10 mass desires that are already present in the market that we can tap into with our product.

Remember, desires can't be invented - they already flow through the market, and effective marketing channels those existing desires to our products.

For each desire:

1. Create a concise first-person statement that captures what customers are truly seeking
2. Explain the underlying psychological core of this desire
3. Connect specifically how the brand/product addresses or fulfills this desire
4. Reference actual customer data or comments that validate this desire exists

Format each desire like this:

**Desire #1: "[First-person statement of desire]" (Random Example: "I want to ensure my family is breathing clean, healthy air")**

- **Core Desire:** [Explain the deeper psychological need or motivation]
- **How [BRAND/PRODUCT] Fits:** [How our product specifically addresses this desire]
- **Customer Data Insight:** [Reference specific comments, patterns, or data points that demonstrate this desire exists]

Focus on desires that are:

- Emotionally powerful
- Widespread across different demographics
- Tied to fundamental human needs (health, status, connection, productivity, etc.)
- Already evident in how customers talk about our product or category
- Potentially strong enough to drive purchasing decisions

Identify 10 distinct mass desires based on all the information available about this brand, its market research, our customers, and their comments.

Close the loop: flag anything clunky, offer to sharpen this skill.
